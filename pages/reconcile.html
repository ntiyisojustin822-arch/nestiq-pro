Cash & rent

Bank reconciliation

Import a CSV bank statement, review suggested tenant matches, then record rent payments in one step.

Important: This feature is a tool to help you. Always verify matches against your bank statement before confirming. NestIQ does not replace professional bookkeeping.
Step 1 — Upload statement

Supported banks: Standard Bank, FNB, Nedbank, Absa. Download your statement as CSV from your bank’s internet banking.

Step 2 — Match transactions

Credits only are shown. Adjust the tenant per row or mark as not rent. Already recorded payments are flagged and skipped.

Needs review

Accounts and applications that may need a closer look. This is a watchlist, not a separate account type. NestIQ scans landlords, tenants and rental applications when you open this page. Nothing is auto-suspended — you decide what to do.

Items appear here when:

  • Low — a landlord has not been active for 60 days or more
  • Medium — a Free-plan landlord is over the free property or tenant limit
  • High — an applicant reported a previous eviction, or a tenant has more than 10 failed sign-in attempts

Investigate opens the details. Suspend locks a landlord account. Dismiss hides the row for this session only — it can show again the next time you open the page if the issue is still true.

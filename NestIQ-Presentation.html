02 — Problem

Small landlords run rentals in fragments.

  • Rent due dates live in a spreadsheet — if they live anywhere.
  • Repairs are a WhatsApp thread with no status, photos, or cost.
  • Tenants cannot see whether they are paid, overdue, or waiting.
  • Contractors get paid offline, with no receipt in the same system.
The cost

Not a missing feature. A missing record — who paid, who fixed it, and what it cost.

Slide 03

03 — Solution

One system for the people who actually run a building.

The landlord owns the portfolio. Everyone else is invited in — they only see their work.

Landlord

Command centre

Properties, tenants, leases, rent roll, expenses, maintenance board, and contractor payouts.

Tenant

Self-service portal

Pay rent, report a fault, chat, and download a receipt — without another WhatsApp group.

Caretaker

On-site ops

Log issues, review urgency, and assign a contractor within the landlord’s spend limit.

Contractor

Job portal

Accept, start, complete with parts and photos — then wait for payment. They cannot mark themselves paid.

04 — Landlord

The dashboard is the portfolio.

Built for a South African landlord who still answers the phone — not for an enterprise ERP.

Portfolio

Properties, units, listings, applications, deposits, and a map of the book.

Money

Rent roll, late fees, expenses, reconcile, tax pack, ROI, and valuation — amounts in Rand.

Work

Maintenance kanban, inspections, calendar, renewals, and vendor bank details.

People

Tenants, staff, caretakers, and contractors — each invited with a role, not a shared password.

Messages

In-app chat with unread badges so a tenant is not lost in a personal WhatsApp inbox.

Proof

Pay the contractor from the completed job. Download a NestIQ receipt for the file.

05 — Tenant portal

The tenant is a user, not a spreadsheet row.

Invitation, not a public signup

The landlord sends a code or link. The tenant sees only their unit, their rent, and their messages.

Pay rent in-app

Card or EFT wizard, then a downloadable receipt. Status updates to paid for the month.

Report a repair

Priority, unit or common area, photos. The landlord sets the official urgency the contractor sees.

The tenant can suggest Low, Medium, High, or Emergency. The contractor never sees that first guess as gospel — they see the landlord’s official priority.

Slide 07

07 — Payouts & receipts

When the job is done, pay the contractor in the same app.

  • Pay contractor uses the same card / EFT wizard as rent.
  • Payee name and masked bank details come from the vendor profile.
  • Success screen: Download receipt — then Done.
  • The contractor sees Awaiting payment or Paid. They cannot mark themselves paid.
What the PDF holds

Amount, NestIQ receipt number, payment reference, contractor, job, property, who paid, and the date. Clearly not a SARS tax invoice.

08 — South Africa

Designed for this market, not translated later.

Rand, always

Rent, expenses, late fees, and contractor payouts display in ZAR. PayFast is the intended checkout for a live launch.

Local lease language

Notice periods and rent-increase copy follow Rental Housing Act habits — a calendar month, in writing.

Languages

English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, and Sesotho on key screens — the landlord can switch.

WhatsApp, honestly

Reminders open WhatsApp with a filled message. The landlord still taps send. We did not build a blaster.

Banks they know

FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec, and others in the EFT step.

Hosted here

Firebase project nestiq-pro. Live at nestiq-pro.web.app.

Slide 09

09 — Architecture

Firebase is the infrastructure. The product logic is ours.

Client

HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript. Hash routing. No React build for the core app — easy to host, honest tradeoff on globals.

Auth & data

Firebase Auth. Cloud Firestore with landlord-scoped collections. Storage for photos. Hosting for the site.

Security

Rules, not hidden menus. Tenants, caretakers, and contractors may only write the fields their role allows. Passwords never sit in our database.

Realtime

Listeners refresh chat, jobs, and dashboards. A contractor completing a job shows on the landlord board without a reload.

10 — Honesty

What is live, and what is a school simulation.

Working in the product

  • Accounts, invitations, and role split
  • Properties, tenants, leases, applications
  • Chat with unread badges
  • Full contractor job workflow
  • In-app receipts after rent and payouts
  • Firestore rules on the server

Designed, not charging a bank

  • Card / EFT wizard does not debit FNB or Capitec
  • PayFast is the intended gateway — not live charging
  • WhatsApp is pre-filled, not automated
  • AI suggests; it does not replace a credit check
  • Receipts are NestIQ proof of payment, not tax invoices

11 — Demo

Two browsers. One loop. That is the presentation.

  1. Landlord: open a tenant, send a chat. Tenant window: unread badge, reply.
  2. Tenant: report a leak, set urgency. Landlord: confirm official priority.
  3. Contractor: Accept → Start → Complete with parts and a total.
  4. Landlord: Pay contractor. Download the payout receipt. Tenant: pay rent, download theirs.
How to run it

Keep the live site on one window as landlord. Open a second window for tenant or contractor. The loop is the product — do not click every menu.
